The provider presenting the price is responsible for it and its own payment terms. This does not waive Moshe's separate mandatory obligations.

## Recommended details and operational work before launch

These are follow-up recommendations, not claims that this content change has implemented the associated systems.

1. **Verify the full business disclosure and service conditions.** Add the actual registered/trading name, business identifier and postal address where required. Confirm the tax/VAT treatment, deposits, waiting allowances, additional-time charges, refund handling and all legally available cancellation channels. Obtain advice on the cancellation rules for each service type and booking arrangement; a contractual 12-hour policy does not replace mandatory law.
2. **Complete a provider and retention inventory.** Record the actual host, email and form-delivery providers, chosen Analytics configuration, advertisers/widgets and any later payment provider. Check their contracts, processing locations, transfer safeguards and retention. Replace broad conditional wording with integration-specific information as each feature is activated, and agree an inbox/booking-record deletion schedule based on real legal duties.
3. **Implement privacy choices before optional tracking where required.** A policy link is not a consent mechanism. Assess applicable Israeli, EEA and UK rules for the actual activity and audience; do not assume every foreign regime automatically applies. Offer meaningful accept/reject/preferences and withdrawal controls for non-exempt technologies. Gate optional scripts and embedded widgets before loading them where required. Consent mode alone should not be mistaken for a complete consent system; some configurations can still send requests after storage is denied.
4. **Review the existing local click journal before public launch.** The current preview feature automatically stores the last 200 outbound-click events locally and has no time-based deletion. It is accurately disclosed, but should be removed, disabled or gated under the applicable rules before launch. This change does not enable new tracking or modify existing collection behavior. Consider self-hosting the current Google Fonts to reduce external requests.
5. **Configure Analytics conservatively when it is added.** Do not send names, emails, telephone numbers, flight details, messages or other enquiry content in ordinary Analytics events, URLs or user identifiers. Review page/query values and automatic form-event capture. Leave user-provided-data/enhanced measurement or advertising features off unless deliberately reviewed, configured and disclosed with any required consent. Set actual retention, access controls and advertising choices, then update the policy accordingly.
6. **Match the forms and any checkout to the documents.** When direct email delivery goes live, update form instructions and the remaining current-draft wording, link the Privacy Policy next to submission, validate/minimise fields, protect the endpoint and never treat a quote request as marketing consent. Before online payments, identify the merchant/processor, ILS total, taxes/fees, booking status and cancellation conditions; use a suitable provider interface rather than collecting card credentials in enquiry messages.
7. **Keep practical records and obtain a final professional review.** Record photo/testimonial permissions, privacy requests, incidents, dated policy versions and accepted booking conditions. Have the final documents and actual launch configuration reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Israeli privacy and consumer law, and applicable overseas obligations. This editorial and implementation review is not a legal-compliance certification.
