## Structural decisions

1. Keep seven sections for now, including a combined Negev & Eilat area; a separate Eilat section with just one shallow card would harm usability.
2. Include cards only when a real, independently navigable existing guide exists. Do not make multiple cards point to one general destination guide just to fill a grid.
3. Cross-link the regional hub and matching category/destination pages through semantic chips; do not insert unrelated arrival routes.
4. Place tour, airport, transport and hotel links in the region's small planning list instead of repeating a generic promotional footer for every attraction.
5. Save all substantive article editing (including legacy comparison, factual updates and content design) for **one dedicated guide at a time**. Review SEO intents and true page depth then.
6. Before publishing new sections, run URL, photo uniqueness and accessibility tests, plus real browser checks on phone and desktop; a passed build is not a visual inspection.
