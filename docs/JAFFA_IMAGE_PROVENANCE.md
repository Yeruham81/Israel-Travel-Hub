## Launch check for remaining archive images

The other local photos were found in the user's legacy travel-photo archive. Metadata inspected so far identifies archive filenames but does not provide a reliable individual creator, contract, public license or photography authorization. They are marked **rights verification pending**, not "free to use" or "verified". Before promoting the site to the main domain, obtain the owner's original licensing/commission records or a written permission record. If unavailable, replace individual photos with legally sourced ones and include the required attribution; never infer or invent photographer credits.

This audit is an internal editorial record, not a technical notice to be displayed to general visitors.
